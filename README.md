# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: No Way Home

> "With great power, there must also come great responsibility." (Aunt May)

## About the Movie
**Spider-Man: No Way Home** is a *superhero action* movie released in **December 2021**, made by Marvel Studios and Sony Pictures.
After his identity is revealed to the world, Peter Parker asks Doctor Strange for help. ~~The spell goes perfectly.~~ The spell goes wrong and pulls villains from other universes into his world.

## Main Characters
1. **Peter Parker / Spider-Man** (Tom Holland): a teenager trying to fix the mess his secret caused
2. **MJ** (Zendaya): Peter's girlfriend, smart and loyal
3. **Doctor Strange** (Benedict Cumberbatch): the sorcerer whose spell breaks the multiverse

## Why I Like It
- It brings three generations of Spider-Man together
  - Tom Holland, Andrew Garfield and Tobey Maguire share the screen
- The villains get a chance at redemption, not just a fight
- The ending is sad but makes Peter a real hero
